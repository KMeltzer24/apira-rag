# Tech Sector Overview — Q2 2026

## Sector themes

1. **AI infrastructure spend** — Hyperscalers (MSFT, GOOGL, AMZN) investing heavily in data centers. Near-term margin pressure, long-term revenue opportunity.

2. **Enterprise AI adoption** — Copilot, Gemini for Workspace, and AWS Bedrock driving B2B software upgrades.

3. **Regulatory headwinds** — Antitrust scrutiny on GOOGL and AAPL app store practices. Monitor but not a thesis-breaker for diversified holders.

## Portfolio construction guidance

| Risk profile | Suggested tech allocation | Top picks |
|-------------|--------------------------|-----------|
| Conservative | 15-20% | MSFT, AAPL |
| Moderate | 25-30% | MSFT, GOOGL, AAPL |
| Aggressive | 30-40% | MSFT, GOOGL, TSLA (small weight) |

## Rebalancing note

If a portfolio is heavy in AAPL and MSFT (common for default tech allocations), consider whether GOOGL adds diversification — it has lower correlation to iPhone/enterprise cycles and stronger AI upside optionality.
