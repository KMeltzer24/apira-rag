# Tesla Inc. (TSLA) — Research Note

**Rating:** Hold (high volatility)

Tesla is a momentum stock with binary outcomes. EV delivery growth has slowed to ~15% YoY as competition intensifies from BYD, legacy automakers, and Chinese brands.

**Bull case:**
- FSD (Full Self-Driving) could unlock software-margin revenue
- Energy storage (Megapack) growing 100%+ YoY
- Robotaxi announcement could re-rate the stock

**Bear case:**
- Margin compression from price cuts (auto gross margin ~18%, down from 25%)
- CEO distraction and governance concerns
- Valuation still premium to traditional automakers (50x+ P/E)

**For diversified portfolios:** TSLA adds volatility, not stability. Existing holders: trim if position exceeds 10% of portfolio. Not recommended as a core holding for conservative investors.
