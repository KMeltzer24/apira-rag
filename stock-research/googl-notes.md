# Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL) — Research Note

**Rating:** Buy

Alphabet offers the best risk/reward in mega-cap tech after lagging peers in 2024. Google Search remains dominant with 90%+ market share, and Google Cloud is now the #3 cloud provider with improving margins.

**AI positioning:**
- Gemini models competitive with GPT-4 class systems
- Search Generative Experience (SGE) could defend search moat
- Waymo autonomous driving as long-term optionality

**Concerns:**
- Antitrust cases in US and EU could force structural remedies
- YouTube ad growth slowing in mature markets

**For portfolios underweight in AI exposure:** GOOGL is a strong add. Current holders: hold and consider modest additions.
