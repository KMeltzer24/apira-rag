# Apple Inc. (AAPL) — Research Note

**Rating:** Hold / slight overweight for diversified tech portfolios

Apple remains a core holding for long-term investors. Services revenue (App Store, iCloud, Apple Pay) continues to grow and now accounts for roughly 25% of total revenue, providing a recurring revenue buffer against iPhone cycle volatility.

**Key strengths:**
- Strong brand loyalty and ecosystem lock-in
- $160B+ annual services revenue run rate
- Consistent dividend growth and aggressive buyback program

**Risks:**
- iPhone revenue concentration (~50% of total)
- Regulatory pressure in EU and US app store markets
- China demand uncertainty

**Recommendation for existing holders:** Maintain position. Consider adding on pullbacks below $170 if you have underweight tech exposure.
