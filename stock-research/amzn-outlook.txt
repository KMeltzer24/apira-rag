Amazon.com Inc. (AMZN) — Research Summary

Outlook: NEUTRAL to SLIGHT BULLISH

AWS remains the cloud infrastructure leader with ~31% market share. Retail margins have improved significantly after the 2022-2023 cost-cutting cycle. Advertising is now a $50B+ run-rate business and the fastest-growing segment.

Positives:
- AWS re-accelerating with AI workload demand
- Retail operating margin expanded to 6%+
- Prime membership stickiness drives recurring revenue

Headwinds:
- Heavy capex spend ($75B+) for AI data centers pressures FCF
- International retail still margin-dilutive

Recommendation: HOLD for existing positions. Not a priority add unless portfolio lacks e-commerce/cloud diversification.
